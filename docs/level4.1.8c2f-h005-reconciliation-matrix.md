## Classification rules applied

- `VERIFIED REMEDIATED` requires current source and focused/regression evidence, not a report alone.
- `PARTIALLY REMEDIATED` means the named boundary has controls, but a related durability, metadata, legacy, or physical-storage boundary remains.
- `NOT APPLICABLE` means source evidence shows the feature is not implemented.
- `UNVERIFIED` means the feature/store could not be established confidently from available source evidence; it is not treated as secure.
